## Corrections

### Foreign import activity chronology

Cross-chat `lastSeenTurn` and `lastWorldActiveTurn` are treated as source-chat clocks rather than portable chronology. `prepareNativeImport` retains the original declared source-chat identity until the public importer applies the bundle. `index.js` determines foreign ownership before target scrubbing and passes it to the canonical `bundle.js` merger. For a foreign import, matching target dossiers preserve their existing target activity counters; newly admitted dossiers receive the target state's current turn as both activity baselines. Same-chat imports keep their existing behavior. No source-message provenance is fabricated, and terminal death, manual archive, and retention protection remain governed by their existing policies.

### Missing/keep durable-profile safety

Missing or `keep` Personality, Speech, and Appearance recovery still requires full established-concept preservation, but now also passes through field-specific safety. Personality uses the identity/morality refinement guard. Speech uses the speech refinement/evolution-language guard. Evolution wording such as `no longer` is rejected before lexical coverage is considered, so repeating old terms inside a negation does not prove their established meaning survived. Both ordinary `npcs` updates and `profileUpdates` use the same field-aware gate. Accepted explicit full-current `refine`, manual locks, development evidence, and the Speech-development ledger remain unchanged.

### Mixed Behavioral Profile reversal safety

Protected agency and morality direction is represented as a bounded direction mask rather than a single polarity that turns mixed evidence into neutral. An established positive direction conflicts with any incoming negative direction, and vice versa, even when the proposal also repeats the established direction under another category. The agency classifier handles the demonstrated `not independent` case explicitly and excludes bounded negated obedience terms from false compliance evidence. Ordinary contextual cooperation or mention of lawful authority does not become obedience by itself. Unsafe full-profile refinements reject atomically and preserve the established list.

### Inferred bond promotion and capacity

Social-edge normalization derives canonical confidence first and retains `inferred` only below explicit authority. Duplicate and reversed-edge promotion now updates `inferred` together with confidence/provenance when stronger evidence wins. Capacity ranking uses canonical confidence instead of letting a stale inferred flag override explicit/manual authority. At the 240-edge cap, only strictly weaker continuity is eligible for eviction; equal or stronger established edges remain protected. Relationship scoring and tie handling are unchanged.
