## Corrections

### Boundary-safe canonical bond storage

Canonical Important Bonds now use one 360-character stored-bond ceiling and a 260-character social-dynamic ceiling. Subject, relation, and dynamic are parsed before limiting. Raw preformatted strings are no longer sliced at the old 220-character dossier limit.

Dynamic normalization operates on deduplicated whole fragments. Complete fragments are retained in order while they fit; a trailing fragment that cannot fit is dropped intact. If one unavoidable fragment is itself too large, it is shortened at a sentence, clause, or word boundary. Word-boundary shortening uses an ellipsis, and canonical output cannot end in a severed word or dangling structural separator.

### One representation across owners

Scanner-edge normalization, hidden social-graph storage, graph-to-dossier projection, ordinary dossier merges, legacy dossier normalization, and explicitly manual/locked Important Bonds all converge through the same canonical bond formatter. Repeated normalization is therefore idempotent instead of progressively shortening already accepted prose.

The larger 360/260 canonical storage budget is deliberately separate from compact scanner and roleplay-injection projections. Model-facing prompt fixtures and request/output budgets remain unchanged.

## Regression coverage

`tests/v118-important-bond-boundaries.test.js` adds five production-function regressions:

1. a 242-character dynamic survives canonical storage beyond the former 220-character ceiling;
2. an over-budget multi-fragment dynamic drops a complete trailing fragment rather than producing shapes such as `authority she resp`;
3. a single oversized dynamic terminates at a readable sentence/clause/word boundary;
4. hidden social-graph projection uses the same boundary-safe representation;
5. repeated normalization remains byte-stable for explicitly locked manual bonds.

The historical deceased-bond compatibility expectation was also updated from legacy `Rook — mentor; deceased` spelling to the already-established canonical `Rook — mentor | deceased` grammar. Production behavior was not changed for that test adjustment.
