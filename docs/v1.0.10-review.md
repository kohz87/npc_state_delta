## Limits

All data and scanners are synthetic. No real SillyTavern chat, database, installed extension, Alpha/Beta repository or paid provider was accessed. These checks do not prove the original live 15-20-message reset, a host-latency explanation, provider extraction quality or device/UI responsiveness. Same-session unsaved recovery remains in memory until a durable save; this hotfix does not add a disk-independent recovery service. The 12 MB journal value remains diagnostic, so required retained history may exceed it. Full branch checkpoints remain separately byte-bounded, and history predating an existing trustworthy baseline cannot be reconstructed. Scanner prompts/schemas, request/retry/output budgets, relationship formulas/gates/fractions, evidence thresholds and Speech locks remain unchanged.
