# NPC State Delta 1.0.28 dossier-profile evolution integrity review

## Scope

Delta 1.0.28 is a deterministic dossier-profile evolution integrity release over the 1.0.27 provider-neutral stable-prefix scanner arrangement. It fixes the six production-function defects reproduced by `dist/profile-evolution-review.mjs` without adding scans, retries, per-field model requests, classifiers, summarizers, background workers, provider-specific cache machinery, or persistence owners.

The fixes retain the existing canonical owners: `core-mechanics.js` for durable profile safety/evidence handling, `appearance.js` for form/current presentation resolution, `social.js` for social graph reconciliation, and `core.js` for facade diagnostics and development-ledger finalization.

## Accepted changes

1. **Durable refine grounding.** Keeping enough established words no longer authorizes unsupported new Personality, Speech, Appearance, or Behavioral Profile meaning. Newly introduced meaning must be grounded in supplied story/evidence. Directly grounded clarification may still apply immediately without being forced through the gradual three-observation threshold. Personality morality/agency protections remain authoritative, including bounded wording such as `enjoys inflicting pain`.

2. **Current Appearance reconciliation.** An accepted flat current appearance update is reconciled into the selected canonical presentation before display resolution. A named form can no longer restore its stale outfit over a valid current update; simultaneous form selection targets the newly selected established form. Unrelated forms, Shared traits, locks, unnamed-current handling, and Stage 4 grounding remain intact.

3. **Shared Appearance deduplication.** Repeated Shared clauses are removed across ordinary punctuation/whitespace boundaries using exact normalized clause/word-sequence matching rather than loose keyword overlap. Distinct detail and negation remain preserved. Resolver, roleplay injection, and portrait projections agree.

4. **Collective Important Bonds.** A collective bond such as `Twin Daughters` retires only when one unique graph group proves the expected complete membership and no unresolved slot remains. Useful group dynamics are carried onto the resolved named edges. Partial resolution preserves the collective remainder; unrelated unresolved bonds, manual locks/deletion, alias promotion, graph confidence, and explicit daughter/mother direction repair remain intact.

5. **Collection reorder/evidence handling.** Mannerism and Behavioral Profile collections are compared order-insensitively. Reorder alone is not semantic development and cannot consume novel evidence. Partial refinements resolve only evidence represented by the accepted final collection; unrelated pending claims remain retained. Diagnostics derive their resolution flags from the canonical accepted collection.

6. **Evidence body coverage.** A matching evidence label is organization metadata, not proof that the observation is represented. Resolution requires substantive body coverage with polarity/negation protection. Novel, contradictory, and genuinely equivalent body cases are covered, while the existing Speech-ledger copied-candidate path still retains novel evidence and reports `waiting-for-revised-candidate`.
