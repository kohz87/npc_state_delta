# NPC State Delta 1.0.24 time-compressed refinement recovery review

## Scope

Delta 1.0.24 is a focused follow-up to the bounded development-episode and diagnostics work in 1.0.23. Live diagnostics proved that elapsed chronology and Sora ownership were being detected correctly, but a provider `gradual` + `refine` result could still remain gated when the provider omitted `developmentReason`, even though field evidence supported a changed candidate. The same diagnostics also exposed aggregate Personality/Speech evidence that was already reflected in the accepted current summary and noisy birthday receipts for unrelated NPCs.

This release does not add another model request, relationship-scoring change, storage-schema migration, native-bundle change, branch-lineage change or background worker.

## Effective development-reason recovery

The Refresh and routine scanner contracts now state that any time-compressed `refine`/`evolve`/`change` must include `developmentReason`. The deterministic backend does not depend solely on provider compliance: when that field is absent, it may derive one effective reason from the field-specific reason or the same field's evidence, in that order.

Evidence recovery is candidate-specific. Personality/Speech still require the changed candidate to be grounded by their field evidence. Mannerisms and Behavioral Profile additionally require every newly proposed entry to be grounded by their own evidence before inferred-batch handling may authorize it. Evidence for an existing curtsy therefore cannot authorize an unrelated new knife-twirling or chalk-tapping mannerism.

The existing bounded time-skip episode, NPC ownership, candidate-change, manual-lock and field-safety gates remain mandatory. Bare elapsed time remains inert.

## Aggregate evidence already reflected

The 1.0.23 field-level aggregate fallback remains conservative: three independent provenance-bearing Personality/Speech observations must collectively ground the candidate. Delta 1.0.24 handles the special case where that aggregate is ready but the provider candidate already equals the accepted current summary. Instead of keeping the fragmented aggregate epoch permanently at `waiting-for-candidate`, Delta records `evidence-already-reflected`, clears the consumed field evidence and resets that aggregate epoch without rewriting the dossier text.

This does not change ordinary concept-ledger readiness. A ready concept ledger with an unchanged candidate still remains `waiting-for-candidate` until a materially changed grounded candidate arrives.

## Diagnostics version 2

The bounded diagnostic export remains noncanonical and always recorded regardless of per-NPC Show/Hide state. Profile receipts now expose candidate-changed/already-represented state, resolved-evidence state, provider-reason presence, effective reason/source, candidate grounding and reason grounding in addition to the retained episode/evidence/application fields.

Birthday receipts no longer appear for every NPC merely because the shared transcript contains birthday language. They are retained only when the NPC has a matched or supplied birthday, an explicit age advance/correction path, or an actual age delta. Relevant rows include bounded previous/current birth date, birth-year source, calendar age, reference date, age state and birth-date state. Birthday arithmetic itself is unchanged from 1.0.22.

## Regression coverage

The 1.0.24 tests cover:

- a Sora-shaped time-compressed Mannerism `refine` with a missing provider `developmentReason` recovering from grounded chalk-tapping evidence;
- rejection when the only available evidence describes a different mannerism than the newly proposed entry;
- fragmented aggregate Speech evidence resolving as already reflected when the provider candidate equals the accepted current Speech;
- preservation of ordinary concept-ledger `waiting-for-candidate` semantics;
- Refresh contract wording requiring a reason for time-compressed refinement;
- birthday diagnostics omitting an unrelated NPC while retaining calendar/birth-year detail for the relevant birthday row;
- all retained 1.0.23 episode/NPC-binding, aggregate, Mannerism/Behavior, diagnostics, birthday and earlier release regressions.

## Prompt accounting

The model-facing change is narrow and measured. Relative to the accepted 1.0.23 Stage 9 fixture, the compact routine scanner and full-window requests are 21 characters smaller, while targeted Refresh is 109 characters larger to make the time-compressed-refine reason contract explicit. Backfill/import/focused-relationship output allowances and roleplay injection remain unchanged. The exact candidate must match the reviewed Stage 9 hashes rather than regenerating them silently.
