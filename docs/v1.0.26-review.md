# NPC State Delta 1.0.26 copied-batch gate-order and targeted-episode review

## Scope

Delta 1.0.26 is a focused follow-up to the copied-candidate/evidence-resolution work in 1.0.25. Live Sora diagnostics showed two remaining deterministic defects: provider-declared `batch` Personality/Speech updates with a copied full candidate could be rejected as `waiting-for-explicit-gate` before candidate/evidence resolution was classified, and targeted Refresh could lose a real long development montage when the nearest elapsed-time anchor and cited `[mN]` evidence were separated by more than the generic 12-segment episode cap.

## Fix

- Provider-declared `batch` Personality/Speech candidates are checked for exact current-field equality before explicit/batch authorization. A copied candidate with novel bounded evidence stays pending as `waiting-for-revised-candidate`; a copied candidate whose evidence is already represented may resolve as `evidence-already-reflected`.
- Candidate/evidence diagnostic flags are populated before later episode gates, so a failed authorization path cannot leave `candidateAlreadyRepresented`, `evidenceAlreadyRepresented`, `evidenceResolved`, or candidate-grounding state misleadingly unset.
- Targeted Refresh may use validated raw `[mN]` provenance to resolve a message-level development episode. Delta finds the nearest prior tagged message containing a narrated elapsed-time anchor before the first cited evidence message, spans through the last cited evidence message, requires sustained-development cues and strong reason grounding across that window, and requires at least one field evidence claim to ground in its own cited source message.
- The targeted bridge activates only when tagged evidence exists. Ordinary/multi-NPC scans keep the existing generic bounded episode window and NPC-isolation rules. No global sentence-cap increase, extra model request, prompt rewrite, relationship change, storage-schema change, bundle change, or branch-lineage change is introduced.

## Regression coverage

`tests/v1026-profile-gate-order.test.js` covers:

1. a copied provider-declared batch Personality candidate whose generic episode gate fails but whose novel evidence must still report `waiting-for-revised-candidate` and remain stored;
2. a copied batch Speech candidate whose evidence is truly represented and may resolve without wording churn; and
3. a targeted `[m202]` -> `[m204]` time-compressed development episode containing more than twenty filler sentence segments, proving the validated message-level path can ground the target without relaxing the generic episode cap.
