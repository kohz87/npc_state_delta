# NPC State Delta 1.0.23 development-episode and diagnostics review

## Scope

Delta 1.0.23 addresses two related durable-profile failures observed in long roleplay montages and adds a bounded diagnostic surface for future troubleshooting. The implementation is generalized and does not special-case any named campaign character.

The release keeps the existing request architecture: no additional model call, no background model worker, no generation barrier, no scanner prompt/schema expansion, no relationship-formula change, no storage-schema bump, no native-bundle-version change, and no branch-lineage change.

## Bounded development episodes

The previous deterministic batch gate localized elapsed development to the time-skip sentence plus its immediately following sentence. That was safe but position-sensitive: a later NPC inside the same coherent montage could lose the shared elapsed-time context.

1.0.23 replaces that sentence pair with a bounded development episode. A recognized elapsed-time anchor opens a capped episode of at most 12 segments / 5,200 characters. A distinct later elapsed episode or an explicit present-scene transition closes it. Overlapping chronology descriptions inside the same montage, such as `in two months`, `over the past month`, and `past seventy days`, do not incorrectly split one episode.

Elapsed time is shared context, not shared authorization. Each evaluated NPC still has to own the relevant development through its name/aliases and bounded field evidence. Another known NPC interrupts pronoun carry; at most one conservative pronoun continuation may extend a named target slice. Candidate grounding may consume only the NPC-scoped episode slice rather than arbitrary transcript history. Targeted Refresh remains one-NPC.

A bare elapsed span remains non-destructive. Inferred batch still requires elapsed time, sustained development, a grounded development reason, a materially changed candidate, candidate grounding, field safety and manual-lock compliance.

## Aggregate gradual Personality/Speech readiness

Concept-ledger readiness remains the preferred ordinary gradual path. 1.0.23 adds a field-level fallback for provider concept fragmentation: at least three independent provenance-bearing observations across the existing minimum span may collectively ground the changed portions of one proposed full Personality/Speech candidate even when their labels differ.

The fallback does not merge concept buckets. Duplicate/replayed sources do not increase readiness. Each independent evidence group must support changed candidate characterization and their union must satisfy the existing candidate-grounding threshold. Unrelated evidence and stale/copied candidates remain blocked.

## Mannerisms and Behavioral Profile

A proven NPC-bound time-compressed episode can now supply the same deterministic inferred-batch rescue to Mannerisms and Behavioral Profile through their existing field gates. Personality/Speech retain their development ledgers. Appearance/form mechanics, relationships, and current-state fields remain separate.

## Always-on bounded diagnostics

`diagnostics-core.js` adds a separate per-chat runtime operation ring with a default retention of 40 operations. Collection is always on. It is not canonical NPC/story state and is not injected, checkpoint-owned characterization, or portable native continuity.

The store uses an explicit allowlist. Retained records may contain compact operation/source identifiers, NPC ids, profile field outcomes, previous/candidate summaries, provider/effective development state, bounded evidence ids/concepts/samples, episode/NPC-binding state, aggregate readiness, locks, candidate grounding, birthday hold/advance results and local token estimates. It intentionally rejects unlisted raw payload fields such as whole story text, full prompts, credentials and provider responses.

Each dossier has Show/Hide Diagnostics. That state is module-local presentation state only and cannot affect recording, scan application, persistence, prompts, rollback, native transfer, or export. Data & Maintenance Diagnostics displays recent retained operations, exports one versioned compact JSON bundle for troubleshooting, and can clear only this runtime diagnostic history.

Birthday diagnostics report deterministic `advance` or `hold`, including calendar rollover, same-day hold, manual locks and the first-establishment guard. The underlying 1.0.22 birthday arithmetic is unchanged.

## Regression coverage

The 1.0.23 tests cover:

- later NPC development several montage segments after an elapsed anchor;
- per-NPC ownership inside one shared time skip;
- cross-NPC authorization rejection;
- separation of distinct elapsed episodes;
- conservative pronoun continuation;
- inferred time-compressed Personality/Speech behavior through retained regressions;
- inferred Mannerisms and Behavioral Profile handling;
- three differently-labelled independent Speech observations reaching aggregate readiness only when they collectively ground the changed candidate;
- unrelated observations staying blocked;
- replay/provenance/lock/cross-chat behavior through the retained ledger suite;
- bounded diagnostic retention and per-NPC filtering;
- export allowlisting that strips raw story/prompt/credential fields;
- per-NPC diagnostic visibility remaining presentation-only;
- Maintenance export/clear hooks;
- birthday advance, idempotent hold, and first-establishment diagnostic receipts;
- all retained 1.0.21 elapsed-span and 1.0.22 birthday/gradual regressions.
