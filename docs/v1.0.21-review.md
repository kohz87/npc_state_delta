# NPC State Delta 1.0.21 natural elapsed-span grounding review

## Scope

Delta 1.0.21 is a focused correction to the deterministic time-compressed-development gate introduced in 1.0.20. A live Gemini 3.7 Flash High Refresh correctly returned `developmentScale: "batch"` and `speechState: "evolve"`, but the backend still rejected the update because natural elapsed phrasing in the supplied story window did not satisfy `hasNarratedTimeSkip()`.

The fix remains provider-independent and adds no model request, retry, background worker, prompt expansion, persisted schema, relationship-scoring change, branch-format change, or native-bundle change.

## Root cause and fix

The 1.0.20 recognizer covered explicit forms such as `three years passed`, `over three years`, `months later`, and named seasonal ranges, but it was too narrow for ordinary narrative forms used by the reported scene: `in two months`, `over the past month`, `the past seventy days`, `for two months`, and `one season under ...`.

1.0.21 generalizes elapsed-duration parsing across day/week/month/year/decade/season units, larger spelled-out quantities, past/previous/last windows, qualified spans, and contextual duration phrases. Age statements remain excluded.

Batch grounding is also stricter than the first attempted correction: the elapsed-time clause or an explicit temporal continuation must contain sustained-development evidence and sufficiently ground the provider's development reason. Weak generic transition words such as unrelated weather `changed` cannot lend their elapsed span to a later one-off profile action. Development-only aliases bridge conservative paraphrases such as schooling/study and training/practice without changing global durable-profile similarity.

The existing field contracts remain intact. Personality/Speech retain the 1.0.19 ledgers and 1.0.20 deterministic batch recovery. Mannerisms and Behavioral Profile continue to use their existing explicit refinement/evolution gates; a grounded time-compressed Refresh can carry those safe refinements alongside Speech without creating a second development ledger for them.

## Regression coverage

The new tests reproduce the reported Ryu shape through production `mergeScanResult`, including explicit `batch` + Speech `evolve`, natural two-month/season/past-day wording, the disappearance of the old guttural speech pattern, and the new Seren-influenced cadence.

Additional coverage verifies:

- `in two months`, including comma-separated narrative form;
- `for two months` and `over the past month`;
- `the past seventy days` and larger spelled-out quantities;
- contextual `one season under ...` phrasing;
- ordinary multi-year development;
- schooling/study and training/practice paraphrase grounding;
- age statements do not count as elapsed development;
- unrelated elapsed weather plus a later one-off polite action cannot authorize batch evolution;
- safe Personality, Mannerisms, and Behavioral Profile refinements can accompany the same grounded time-compressed Refresh.
